The whole integration

One line. No access to their backend.

A retailer adds a single tag to their site. Mirror finds the product images, adds a “Try it on” button to each, and does the rest. That’s the entire lift.

<script src="https://your-mirror-server.com/mirror.js" defer></script>

It works even on pages that were never told about Mirror — it can find the product images on its own — so you can demo it on a prospect’s real store, not just a mock-up.

Straight answer

What’s real in this preview, and what isn’t.

This pageThe full interface and flow are real and interactive. The generated “look” here is a styled sample made in your browser — the true try-on is produced by AI on the server.
The real thingRuns on the app in your repo. It sends the shopper’s photo + the garment to Google Gemini and returns a photorealistic try-on in about 5–15 seconds.
Your photoStays in your browser here. In the real app it’s sent to the AI to make the preview and is never stored on the server.
Fail-safeIf the AI is ever down, it quietly shows a sample instead of an error — the store never looks broken.
Test the real AI

Run the full app in three steps.

  1. Get the codePull the main branch of your Outfit-application repo to a computer with Node 18+.
  2. Start itRun npm install then npm start, and open localhost:3000. It works immediately with sample results — no key needed.
  3. Switch on real AIPut a free Gemini key in .env (aistudio.google.com/apikey) and restart. Now every try-on is generated live from a real photo.
